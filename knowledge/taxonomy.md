# Taxonomy

The domains of this knowledge base. A domain is a top-level folder under `knowledge/` that groups one subject
area. Check this list before creating a folder, and add the domain here first.

| Domain | Covers | Does not cover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `knowledge` (meta) | The index, this taxonomy, and the glossary | Subject-matter knowledge |

To decide where content belongs: if a passage would still hold for another component, it is general knowledge
and belongs to the domain that owns that subject; keep only what is specific in the more specific domain.
