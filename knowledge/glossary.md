## Knowledge base

- Aliases: KB, knowledge
- Definition: The documents under `knowledge/`, except `knowledge/sources/`, that keep the team's own
  long-lived, reusable knowledge.
- Notes: Scratch work is not part of it. External originals and summaries of them live in
  `knowledge/sources/`.
